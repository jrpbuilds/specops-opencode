# Code of Conduct

SpecOps aims to be a friendly, professional open-source project.

## Be reasonable

When participating in the project:

- Be respectful of other contributors.
- Give constructive feedback and assume good intent.
- Keep discussions focused on the project.
- Don't harass, threaten, discriminate against, or deliberately antagonise others.
- Don't publish somebody else's private information.

Disagreement is fine. Personal attacks and harassment aren't.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may edit, remove, or reject comments, issues, pull requests, or other
contributions that violate these expectations.

Repeated or serious violations may result in being blocked from participating in
the project.

If you need to report a conduct issue privately, contact the project maintainer.
